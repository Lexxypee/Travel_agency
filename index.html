About Us

At AmenTravel&Tour, we believe that travel should be about creating unforgettable memories, not worrying about logistics. Founded in 2010, our mission is to provide exceptional travel experiences tailored to your unique needs.

What sets us apart is our personalized approach. We don't just book trips; we craft experiences. Our team of travel experts has visited every destination we offer, ensuring we can provide firsthand recommendations and insights.

From the moment you contact us until you return home, we're with you every step of the way, ensuring a seamless and stress-free journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

We recommend booking at least 3-6 months in advance for international trips, especially during peak seasons. For last-minute bookings, we'll do our best to accommodate you, but options may be limited.

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), bank transfers, and in some cases, PayPal. A deposit is typically required to secure your booking, with the balance due 60 days before departure.

We understand plans change. Most changes can be accommodated with notice, though fees may apply depending on timing and supplier policies. We offer travel insurance options that cover cancellations for covered reasons.

Our process is simple: 1) Initial consultation to understand your needs, 2) We create a customized itinerary, 3) You review and request adjustments, 4) Finalize details and book, 5) We handle all logistics while you prepare for your adventure. We're with you every step of the way!
